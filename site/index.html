Why Riser

Snoozing feels good for nine minutes. Riser makes getting up feel better. Do ten push-ups, find one thing around the house, or crack a little math, and a tiny sprout heads off to work, because you got up.

01 · The alarm

Wake up with a mission.

When the alarm rings, tap Start Mission. Your camera counts push-ups or squats live, or go hunt for one thing around the house. It stops when you're really up.

02 · The payoff

Your buddy heads off to work.

Win the morning and your buddy rides a balloon to the Farm Island for a shift, harvesting the crops that only grow when you get up. Every shift earns coins and XP, with a promotion the more mornings you win together.

03 · The world

Build your little world.

Spend coins on 30 things to build and place them anywhere. Buy new isles across rope bridges, and decorate a cozy room from a market that changes every day.

04 · The night

Then, sleep on time.

Riser counts back five sleep cycles from your alarm. At bedtime your buddy walks inside and goes to sleep, and gently suggests you do too.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Will it ring if my phone is on Silent?

Yes. Riser uses Apple's AlarmKit, the same system alarms as the Clock app, so it rings through Silent mode and Focus.

What if I can't do push-ups?

Pick squats, an Item Hunt or a quick brain warm-up instead. Only do movements you're comfortable with.

Does Riser record me?

No. Camera frames are analysed in memory on your iPhone by Apple's Vision framework and are never saved or uploaded.

What happens if I miss a morning?

Your buddy has a gloomier day and the crops wilt, and after a few misses your buddy is sick in bed. One good morning makes it better, and everything you've built stays yours.

How do I cancel?

Any time in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel during the free trial and you won't be charged.
