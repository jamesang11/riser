# Riser: game design

**Premise:** your alarm is your sprout's alarm. When you get up, he goes to work. When you don't, things fall apart, slowly and visibly, but always recoverably.

## Daily loop
1. **Night:** Sprig goes to bed at the bedtime calculated from your alarm (5 sleep cycles + 15 min). Decorate his home, buy hats and decor.
2. **Alarm:** it won't stop until you finish a mission (push-ups and squats counted by the camera, a walk, or math). Escape-proof mode re-rings if you cheat.
3. **Commute cutscene:** a balloon ride to the Farm Island. Sprig runs the farm himself: he harvests what's ripe, replants and waters.
4. **Shift report:** crops sold + wage (career rank + streak) × mood multiplier. Wages pay off any debt first.
5. **Day:** he works the farm (visible from home via the Home | Farm switcher) and rides home in the evening.
6. **Days off** (no alarm): he goes adventuring by himself late morning and brings back a postcard (8 destinations, 48 discoveries in the Journal logbook).

## Resources
| Resource | Earned by | Spent on / lost by |
|---|---|---|
| Coins | Shifts (harvest + wage), perfect weeks, adventures | Buildings, hats, furniture, room styles, weekly upkeep |
| XP / Level | Every won morning | Unlocks decor, destinations, sprout growth stages |
| Career rank | 1 point per shift worked | Demoted after 2 misses in a row |
| Mood (0–100) | Won mornings (+20), petting | Missed mornings (−30). Sets pay: happy ×1.2, sad ×0.7 |
| Streak | Consecutive won mornings | Any miss (unless a Riser+ streak freeze absorbs it) |

## Career ladder
Farmhand 🧺 (wage 10) → Sprout Grower 🌱 (5 shifts, 14) → Harvester 🥕 (12, 19) → Foreman 📋 (25, 26) → Head Farmer 🧑‍🌾 (45, 34) → Farm Owner 🏡 (75, 45). Promotions arrive as letters from Farmer Figg.

## Neglect ladder (consecutive missed alarm mornings)
| Misses | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1 | Crops wilt (growth paused), mood −30, no pay, warning letter from Farmer Figg |
| 2 | Wilted crops wither, **demotion**, a small **dark cloud** follows Sprig around, light dims |
| 3 | **Sick:** green, nausea swirls, nightcap, sneezes, sad face. The next shift is a half-pay recovery shift |
| 5 | **Sick in bed:** the view moves inside his tent/bedroom and you can't leave. No work, no income. **One** won morning gets him out of bed |

Also:
- **Weekly upkeep:** 5 + 3 coins per building, charged each new week. If you can't pay, it becomes **debt**: the island turns dusty and washed-out, all shops close, and wages go to the debt first.
- **Never lost:** levels, decor, hats and logbook progress. Everything can be won back by waking up.

## Positive loops
- **Perfect week:** every alarm morning of the week won → +40 plus 10 coins per alarm day.
- **Harvest Festival (October):** a chest every festival morning with exclusive decor and hats.
- **Daily hat market** and adventure-exclusive hats.
- **Home interiors:** furniture and wall/floor/canvas styles from the daily market.

## Building & islands
- **Free placement.** Buying something opens a see-through preview. Drag it anywhere, tap a spot, or rotate it in 45° steps. A green ring means it fits; a red ring means it overlaps something, sits off the edge, or blocks the front door. You pay on "Build here".
- **Moving.** Tap anything on the island (or pick it in Build) and choose **Move**. The home (tent/cottage) stays put so the door and path always line up.
- **More decor.** Flower bushes, mossy boulders, signpost, beehives, picnic table, wishing well, swing, flower arch, hammock, fountain, gazebo and lighthouse (Lv 1–7, 25–260 coins).
- **Expansion isles.** Buy islands from Build › Isles; each is joined to home by a rope bridge:
  - **Meadow Isle:** Lv 4, 500 coins.
  - **Sandy Isle:** Lv 7, 1,000 coins.

  Each isle gets its own button in the place switcher, and new builds default to the isle you're looking at. The sprout lives on the home island, and only home items affect his walking paths.
- Achievements: Island Hopper (buy a second island), Archipelago (own them all).

## Riser+
+50% wage, premium decor and furniture, a weekly streak freeze (absorbs one missed morning entirely), unlimited alarms, extra sounds, and 6 market items a day.
